Does this apply to you?

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is phased in by qualifying income — your gross self-employment and property income added together, before expenses.

If you are under the threshold for your year, you have nothing to file quarterly yet — carry on with your normal Self Assessment return. Plenty of people are being sold software for a deadline that is still two years away from them.

Which dates does your update cover?

There are two sets of update periods. The deadlines are identical — only the period covered changes. Pick whichever matches how you keep your books.

The bit almost everyone gets wrong: each update is cumulative from the start of the tax year, not just the last three months. Your November update covers 6 April to 5 October, not July to October. That is deliberate — it means you fix a mistake by correcting your records, not by resubmitting an old quarter.

Three things that catch people out

A spreadsheet on its own will not file it. HMRC does not accept a spreadsheet as a submission. You either keep records in MTD-compatible software, or you keep them in a spreadsheet and pair it with bridging software that carries the totals across. Anyone selling you a spreadsheet as "HMRC approved filing" is selling you a problem.
A nil quarter still has to be filed. No income and no expenses in the period does not mean no update. You send it anyway to tell HMRC there was nothing.
Penalty points are switched off for 2026/27 — but the update is not optional. HMRC will not apply late-submission penalty points for quarterly updates during the 2026/27 tax year. That is breathing room, not an exemption: you still cannot submit your tax return until the quarterly updates are in, and late-return penalties apply as normal.
